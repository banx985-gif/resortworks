RESORTWORKS Privacy Policy

Banx Games · Effective 8 November 2026

The short version

  • Your game is saved on your own phone or computer. We never receive it.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We don't collect your name, email or any other personal data.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your phone's advertising ID to pick them, and you can choose general ads instead.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

RESORTWORKS is a resort management game made by Banx Games, a small independent developer. This policy covers the RESORTWORKS app for Android and the RESORTWORKS web version. If you have any question about it, email banx985@gmail.com.

What the game stores

RESORTWORKS saves your resorts, your settings and your answer about ads in the storage of the device you play on. This data stays on that device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive it.

You can remove it at any time: delete a save slot in the game, uninstall the app, or clear the app's (or the web page's) stored data. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your saves are gone for good, because no copy exists anywhere else.

No account, no personal data from us

RESORTWORKS has no account, no sign-in and no chat. We (Banx Games) don't collect, receive or sell any personal data, and the game contains no analytics or tracking of our own.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a small in-game reward, and an occasional break-time ad at the end of a game month (never before your first award). The web version shows no real ads.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob software in the app may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your phone's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and approximate location worked out from it,
  • which ads were shown and whether you tapped them,
  • basic device and app information, and crash or performance details about the ads.

This information goes to Google, not to us. On first launch the app asks how ads may be picked: you can allow ads that may be personalised using your advertising ID, or choose general (non-personalised) ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland this question is Google's own consent form instead, and no ad is requested before you answer it. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & legal → Ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Security & privacy → Ads on newer phones).

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads and two fixed packs of Resort Tokens — one-time purchases, no subscriptions) are sold and handled by Google Play. You pay Google, under Google Play's terms, and your payment details are never shared with us. The app only receives Google Play's confirmation of what you bought, and keeps that on your device so your purchase works. Refunds are managed in the Google Play app. Google's privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

The game is fully playable without paying. Nothing is sold in random packs or loot boxes.

The web version

The web version is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server records (such as your IP address and browser type) when the game's files are loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

RESORTWORKS is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help.

Phone features the app uses

The app uses your phone's vibration for feedback and keeps the screen on while the game clock is running. It uses the internet only to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Notifications

If you say yes when the game asks (never at first launch), RESORTWORKS can show a few reminders on your phone or in your browser, such as when a build is nearly done, an event is coming up or a reward is waiting. They are made and scheduled on your device (at most 3 a day, never between 10 pm and 8 am); nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and no personal data is used. You can switch them off, all together or one kind at a time, in Settings → Notifications.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Big changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
